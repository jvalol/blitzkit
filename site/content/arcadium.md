# capriccio

A building that could not stand up in the real world, with a cabinet in the
hall for every game built on [blitzkit](https://blitzkit.jva.lol). It runs in
this tab, on your graphics card. Nothing is installed and nothing is kept.

Past the last cabinet the hall opens into a nook, and past that there is a
pool, a hot tub, a sauna and a zen garden. Explore away.

**W A S D** to walk, the mouse to look, and click once to let it take the
mouse. **Return** uses whatever you are looking at. **Tab** lists what is out
past the door. Hold **Escape** to leave.

The cabinets are scenery. Starting one means running a program. Four of the games run in a browser on their own
page: [starry, snake, slider and poolhall](https://blitzkit.jva.lol/docs/games/).
