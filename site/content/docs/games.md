# Games

Five games run on blitzkit. Each one is its own repository, and each depends on
the published crate rather than a local checkout, so the engine gets used the
way anyone else would use it.

## pong

In which i develop a pong game in rust using blitzkit, also an open repo
currently in its early stages.

[github.com/jvalol/pong](https://github.com/jvalol/pong)

![Pong: two paddles, the ball mid flight, and the score at the top](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jvalol/pong/main/media/screenshot.png)

## snake

In which I develop a snake game using blitzkit.

[github.com/jvalol/snake](https://github.com/jvalol/snake)

![Snake: a nine segment snake winding through two turns in the lower left of the board, with a pellet away to the upper right](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jvalol/snake/main/media/screenshot.png)

## tetris

The third game on the engine, after pong and snake.

[github.com/jvalol/tetris](https://github.com/jvalol/tetris)

![Tetris: a piece falling, the next one queued beside the well, and the score, level and rows cleared](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jvalol/tetris/main/media/screenshot.png)

## marble

Roll a marble across a course of platforms to the goal, against a clock. The
first 3D game built on blitzkit.

[github.com/jvalol/marble](https://github.com/jvalol/marble)

![Marble: the marble on the starting pad, the course of platforms receding to the green goal, gems floating above them](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jvalol/marble/main/media/screenshot.png)

## slider

Fly down a tunnel that wanders, through fourteen rings strung along it. You
speed up the further you get, from 22 metres a second to 70. Scraping the wall
costs you, missing a ring costs you, and both cost you the same way: time.

[github.com/jvalol/slider](https://github.com/jvalol/slider)

![Looking down a checkered tunnel bending hard to the right, with a gold ring hanging in the middle distance](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jvalol/slider/main/media/screenshot.png)
